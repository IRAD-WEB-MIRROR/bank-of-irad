Licensed institutions

The Bank of Irad licenses and supervises commercial banks, microfinance institutions and currency exchange offices in the Irad Special Economic Zone. Only the institutions in this register may take deposits or accept payments from the public.

…

Currency exchange offices

The register of licensed currency exchange offices is being updated. Until it is published, currency should be exchanged at the branches of the licensed banks above, at the official rate.

Not licensed

Money changers in markets and in the street, and payment services offered to residents without a licence (see Notice 2026/5), do not appear in this register. Balances and cash entrusted to them carry no protection.

Forms and procedures

  • Open a currency exchange booth: every step, on e-Irad
  • Payment order for state duty, BI-0401 (PDF)
  • Check a firm in the Register of Firms
