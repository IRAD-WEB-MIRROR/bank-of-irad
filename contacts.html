Contacts and reception

The head office of the Bank is on Liberty Boulevard in Talas. The Bank does not answer enquiries about prices quoted by third parties, and it does not give advice on individual investments.

Written appeals

Written appeals may be sent by post to the head office, handed in at the Reception Office or sent by e-mail to info@bank.iradgov.org. Give your full name, postal address and the subject of the appeal. Appeals are answered within 30 days, under Order No. 41 of 9 April 2019.

Complaints about a bank or a microfinance institution are considered only after the institution itself has replied, or has not replied within 15 days. Attach its reply.

How to get here

Liberty Boulevard 3 is in the centre of Talas. Visitors to the cash hall use the main entrance; the Reception Office and the Library are reached from the side entrance on Bank Lane. There is no visitors' car park.
