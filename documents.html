Documents and legal acts

The Bank works under the Banking and Commercial Currency Act 1993 and the Bank of Irad Act 2008 (see The currency board). Below are the Bank's own resolutions, regulations and orders that concern banks and the public. Notices of the Bank in force are published under Notices and press releases. The authoritative text is the English text.

Order of the Chairman · 9 April 2019 · No. 41

On the reception of citizens and the consideration of written appeals

In force

Who receives citizens and when, how written appeals (including e-mail) are registered, and the 30-day time limit for replies.

Full text ›

Regulation of the Board · 20 November 2018 · No. R-2018/4

On the exchange of worn and damaged banknotes and coins

In force

When a worn or damaged banknote or coin is exchanged at its full value, and how to hand it in. In force from 1 December 2018.

Full text ›

Order of the Chairman · 2 May 2017 · No. 22

Rules of the Library and Reading Room

In force

Opening days, appointments and the use of the Bank's printed publications in the Reading Room.

Full text ›

Order of the Chairman · 14 September 2015 · No. 58

Code of conduct for employees of the Bank of Irad

In force

Confidentiality, conflicts of interest, gifts and dealings in currency by the Bank's staff.

Full text ›

Resolution of the Board · 5 June 2012 · No. 2012/9

Operating Rules of the Bank of Irad Clearing House

In force

Rules for the member banks: settlement accounts with the Bank, real-time settlement of payments in dollars and som, the daily schedule, operating days (Monday to Friday except public holidays) and the backup site in Nurzhan. Changes to the operating days are announced by notice, for example Notice 2026/6. Issued to the member banks.

Resolution of the Board · 1 March 2011 · No. 2011/3

On the issue of banknotes and coins of the 2011 series

In force

Puts into circulation the banknotes of 1, 5, 10, 20, 50, 100 and 500 som and the coins of 10 and 50 tyiyn and of 1 and 5 som of the 2011 series, and describes their designs and security features. The designs are shown on the page Banknotes and coins.
