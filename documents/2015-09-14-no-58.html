Order of the Chairman · 14 September 2015 · No. 58

Code of conduct for employees of the Bank of Irad

In force

1. Employees of the Bank keep confidential the information about banks and their customers that they learn in their work, during their employment and after it.

2. An employee who has a personal interest in a matter he or she deals with, including through a relative, tells his or her head of department in writing and takes no part in the decision.

3. Employees do not accept gifts, payments or hospitality from licensed institutions or their customers, other than items of small value given at official events.

4. Employees buy and sell foreign currency only at licensed banks and at the official rate. They take no part in the buying or selling of currency by unlicensed persons.

5. Employees do not speak to the press on behalf of the Bank unless authorised by the Press Office.

6. Breaches of this Code are considered by a commission appointed by the Chairman.

Chairman of the Bank of Irad. Talas, 14 September 2015.
