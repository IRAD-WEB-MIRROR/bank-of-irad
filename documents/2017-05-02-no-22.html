Order of the Chairman · 2 May 2017 · No. 22

Rules of the Library and Reading Room

In force

1. The Library and Reading Room of the head office keeps the publications of the Bank of Irad, including the Annual Reports and the Statistical Bulletins, for public reference.

2. The Reading Room is open on Tuesdays and Thursdays from 10:00 to 12:00. Readers make an appointment in advance by telephone (+996-39-2-21-40-27) and name the publications they wish to consult.

3. Readers show an identity document at the side entrance. Bags and coats are left in the cloakroom.

4. Publications are consulted in the Reading Room only and are not lent. Readers may copy short extracts by hand; photocopies are made by the librarian, not more than twenty pages for each reader on each visit.

5. The Library does not answer enquiries by telephone about figures in the publications. Written enquiries are dealt with as appeals.

A. Temirov, Chairman. Talas, 2 May 2017.
