Regulation of the Board · 20 November 2018 · No. R-2018/4

On the exchange of worn and damaged banknotes and coins

In force

1. This Regulation sets the rules under which the Bank of Irad exchanges som banknotes and coins that are worn or damaged in circulation.

2. A banknote is exchanged at its full value if more than half of its area remains and both serial numbers, or one serial number and the watermark, can be identified. Notes that are worn, torn, stained, written on or repaired with tape are exchanged on the same terms.

3. A banknote of which half or less remains, or which is burnt, chemically damaged or stuck together with other notes, is sent for examination by the Currency Issue and Cash Department. The decision is given in writing within 30 days.

4. A coin is exchanged at its full value if its value and design can be identified. Coins that are bent, cut or melted are not exchanged.

5. Banknotes and coins are handed in at the cash hall of the head office or at any branch of a licensed bank. The bank pays the full value of notes covered by paragraph 2 at once and sends the other notes to the Bank of Irad with the customer's application.

6. No commission is charged for an exchange under this Regulation.

7. A banknote or coin that appears to be counterfeit is not exchanged. It is kept by the bank, the customer is given a receipt, and the note or coin is sent to the Bank of Irad for examination.

8. This Regulation comes into force on 1 December 2018.

Approved by the Board of the Bank of Irad. Talas, 20 November 2018.
