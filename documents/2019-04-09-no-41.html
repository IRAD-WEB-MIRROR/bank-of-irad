Order of the Chairman · 9 April 2019 · No. 41

On the reception of citizens and the consideration of written appeals

In force

In order to set a single procedure for dealing with citizens, I ORDER:

1. The Chairman receives citizens on the last Thursday of each month from 15:00 to 17:00, and the Deputy Chairman on the second Wednesday of each month from 14:00 to 16:00. Appointments are made through the Reception Office, by telephone or in person.

2. The Banking Supervision and Licensing Department receives citizens with complaints about licensed institutions on Mondays and Wednesdays from 10:00 to 12:00.

3. Written appeals received by post, handed in at the Reception Office or sent to info@bank.iradgov.org are registered on the day they are received and passed to the department concerned.

4. A reply is given within 30 days of registration. If the matter needs further inquiry, the citizen is told so in writing and the time may be extended by no more than 30 days.

5. Appeals that give no name or address, and appeals about prices quoted by third parties, are not answered.

6. Order No. 17 of 12 March 2009 is repealed.

A. Temirov, Chairman. Talas, 9 April 2019.
