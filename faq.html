Questions and answers

Questions the Bank is asked most often. Questions about the fixed rate and the reserves are answered on the page The currency board.

Banknotes and coins

I have a torn or damaged banknote. Can it be exchanged?

Yes. A banknote is exchanged at its full value if more than half of it remains and its serial number can be identified. Other cases are examined by the Bank. See the Regulation on the exchange of worn and damaged banknotes and coins.

Where do I hand in a damaged banknote?

At any branch of a licensed bank or at the cash hall of the head office, Monday to Friday, 09:00–16:00. No commission is charged.

How do I check a banknote?

Look at it against the light for the watermark and the see-through tündük, feel the raised print and look for the windowed thread. The features of each note are described on the page Banknotes and coins.

What should I do if I think a banknote is counterfeit?

Do not pass it on. Hand it in at a bank, which will give you a receipt and send the note to the Bank of Irad for examination. If the note is genuine, its value is paid to you.

Are tyiyn coins still legal tender?

Yes. The coins of 10 and 50 tyiyn are legal tender, like the som notes and the coins of 1 and 5 som.

May I print pictures of banknotes in an advertisement or a book?

Only with the written permission of the Bank. Pictures must be clearly smaller or larger than the real note, printed on one side only and marked SPECIMEN. Write to the Press Office.

Banks and complaints

How do I know that a bank is licensed?

The banks and microfinance institutions licensed by the Bank are listed in the register of licensed institutions. Exchange currency only at licensed banks.

I have a complaint about my bank. Can the Bank of Irad help?

First write to the bank itself. If it does not reply within 15 days, or you disagree with its reply, write to the Banking Supervision and Licensing Department and attach the bank's reply. The Bank checks whether the bank has kept the rules; it does not decide private disputes, which go to the courts.

Does the Bank of Irad open accounts or exchange currency for the public?

No. The Bank keeps accounts only for the licensed banks. Accounts, transfers and currency exchange are provided by the licensed banks.

Is a digital token or cryptocurrency legal tender?

No. Only the som and the United States dollar are legal tender in the ISEZ. See the statement of 24 September 2026.

The Bank

How can I consult the Bank's publications?

The publications are issued in print and may be consulted at the Library and Reading Room on Tuesdays and Thursdays, 10:00–12:00, by appointment (+996-39-2-21-40-27). They are listed under Publications and statistics.

Can a school class visit the Bank?

Yes. The Press Office arranges visits and lessons for school pupils and students. Write to press@bank.iradgov.org at least a month ahead.

How do I make an appointment with the Chairman?

Call the Reception Office on +996-39-2-21-40-05 or come in person to the side entrance on Bank Lane. Reception days are given on the page Contacts and reception.

How do I apply for a job at the Bank?

Posts are filled by open competition and announced on the page Vacancies.
