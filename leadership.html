Leadership

The Bank of Irad is directed by its Board, headed by the Chairman. The names of the other members of the Board are given in the Annual Report. See also About the Bank.

Azamat Temirov

Chairman of the Bank of Irad · since 2010

Chairman since 2010. Deputy Chairman of the Bank from 2007 to 2010 and one of the authors of the reset of 2008, which kept the currency-board rules and set the rate at 8.40 som to the dollar.

From 1997 to 2006 he was a consultant to the World Bank on Central Asian financial-sector projects, and he returned to Talas in 2007. He oversees the correspondent banking arrangements in Almaty and Istanbul through which Iradi banks make payments abroad. His signature is on the banknotes of the 2011 series.
