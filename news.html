Press service news

2025

  • 14 October 2025
    New equipment at the Nurzhan backup site

    The Payment Systems Department has completed the replacement of the equipment at the Clearing House's backup site in Nurzhan. The work was done over three weekends and did not interrupt settlement.

  • 24 June 2025
    Statistical Bulletin 2024 published

    The 2024 issue of the Statistical Bulletin has been published in print. It gives the som in circulation and reserve cover by month, a summary of the licensed banks' balance sheets and the turnover of the Clearing House.

    Annual Report 2023 published

2023

  • 14 November 2023
    Tyiyn coins remain legal tender

    The Bank of Irad reminds the public that the coins of 10 and 50 tyiyn are legal tender, like the som notes and the coins of 1 and 5 som. They are accepted at the cash hall and at the licensed banks.

  • 20 June 2023
    Statistical Bulletin 2022 published

    The Bank has published the 2022 issue of its Statistical Bulletin: the som in circulation and reserve cover by month, the balance sheets of the licensed banks in summary and the turnover of the Clearing House.

2022

  • 18 October 2022
    Lessons on money for school pupils

    In October staff of the Bank gave lessons on the som, the dollar and the work of banks in eight schools in Talas and Nurzhan. Pupils learned how to check a banknote and why only licensed banks may exchange currency.

  • 15 March 2022

2021

  • 18 May 2021
    Ten years of the 2011 series: check your banknotes

    Ten years ago the Bank began to issue the banknotes and coins of the 2011 series, which added the 500-som note and put the portrait of Amanbek Alimbekov on the 100-som note.

  • 9 April 2019
    Written appeals to the Bank may now be sent by e-mail

    By Order No. 41 the Chairman has set new rules for the reception of citizens and for written appeals. Appeals sent by e-mail to info@bank.iradgov.org are now registered in the same way as letters and answered within 30 days.
