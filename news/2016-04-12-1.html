12 April 2016

Annual Report 2015 published

The Bank of Irad has published its Annual Report for 2015. The report gives the reserve cover and the som in circulation at the end of the year and reviews banking supervision and the work of the Clearing House.

The report is issued in print. Copies have been sent to the licensed banks and may be consulted at the Library and Reading Room of the head office on Tuesdays and Thursdays.
