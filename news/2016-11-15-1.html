15 November 2016

Twenty years of the Clearing House

The Bank of Irad Clearing House, the interbank payment system of the ISEZ, was set up in 1996. It first settled cheques and batch payments once a day; today large dollar payments and som payments are settled in real time, with backup sites in Nurzhan.

To mark the anniversary the Bank received representatives of the member banks at the head office and thanked the staff of the Payment Systems Department who have kept the system working since 1996, including through the crisis of 2006–2007.
