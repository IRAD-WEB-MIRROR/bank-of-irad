16 May 2017

The Library and Reading Room reopens

The Library and Reading Room of the head office has reopened after repairs. It keeps the Bank's Annual Reports, Statistical Bulletins and other publications for public reference.

The Reading Room is open on Tuesdays and Thursdays from 10:00 to 12:00 by appointment, under the new Rules of the Library and Reading Room. Appointments: +996-39-2-21-40-27.
