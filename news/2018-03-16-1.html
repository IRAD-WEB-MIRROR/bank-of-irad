16 March 2018

Ten years of the Bank of Irad Act: round table at Talas State University

On the tenth anniversary of the Bank of Irad Act 2008 the Bank and the economics faculty of Talas State University held a round table on the currency board. Staff of the Bank, teachers and students discussed the rules under which the som is issued and the reset of 2008.

Chairman A. Temirov opened the round table. The rate of 8.40 som to the dollar, set on 17 March 2008, is unchanged, and the som remains fully covered by the Bank's reserves.
