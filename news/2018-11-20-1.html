20 November 2018

New rules for the exchange of worn and damaged banknotes

The Board of the Bank has approved a new Regulation on the exchange of worn and damaged banknotes and coins, in force from 1 December 2018.

A banknote that is worn, torn or stained is exchanged at its full value if more than half of it remains. Licensed banks accept such notes from their customers and send them to the Bank; doubtful cases are examined by the Currency Issue and Cash Department.
