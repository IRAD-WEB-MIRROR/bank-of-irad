9 April 2019

Written appeals to the Bank may now be sent by e-mail

By Order No. 41 the Chairman has set new rules for the reception of citizens and for written appeals. Appeals sent by e-mail to info@bank.iradgov.org are now registered in the same way as letters and answered within 30 days.

The Chairman and the Deputy Chairman receive citizens once a month by appointment through the Reception Office.

Press Office of the Bank of Irad
