22 October 2019

Clearing House exercise at the Nurzhan backup site

On 19 October the Payment Systems Department carried out its annual exercise in which settlement is moved from the head office to the backup site in Nurzhan and back. All member banks took part. Payments were settled without delay.

The exercise was held on a Saturday so that customers were not affected.

Press Office of the Bank of Irad
