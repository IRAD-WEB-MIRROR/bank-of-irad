20 March 2020

Cash hall and Clearing House during the quarantine regime

While the quarantine regime is in force, the cash hall of the head office is open from 10:00 to 13:00 and serves banks and visitors by appointment only. The Library and Reading Room and the reception of citizens are suspended; written appeals are accepted by post and e-mail.

The Clearing House is working normally. Salaries, pensions and payments between banks are settled as usual.

Press Office of the Bank of Irad
