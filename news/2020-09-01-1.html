1 September 2020

Cash hall returns to normal hours

From 1 September 2020 the cash hall is open again from 09:00 to 16:00, Monday to Friday. The Library and Reading Room reopens on Tuesdays and Thursdays by appointment, with no more than four readers at a time.

Personal reception of citizens by the Chairman and the Deputy Chairman resumes in October.
