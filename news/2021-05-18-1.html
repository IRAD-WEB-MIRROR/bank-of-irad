Ten years of the 2011 series: check your banknotes

Ten years ago the Bank began to issue the banknotes and coins of the 2011 series, which added the 500-som note and put the portrait of Amanbek Alimbekov on the 100-som note.

The Bank reminds the public how to check a banknote: look for the watermark and the bright numeral against the light, the windowed thread, the see-through tündük, the raised print and, on the 20 and 50 som, the numeral that changes from gold to green. The features are described on the page Banknotes and coins.

Press Office of the Bank of Irad
