15 March 2022

Transfers from abroad continue through correspondent banks

In reply to enquiries from the public, the Bank of Irad confirms that transfers from abroad continue to be paid to customers of Iradi banks in dollars or som. Payments from abroad are settled through correspondent banks in Almaty and Istanbul.

Commissions and the time a transfer takes are set by the transfer services and the banks. Customers should ask their bank before sending or receiving a transfer.

Press Office of the Bank of Irad
