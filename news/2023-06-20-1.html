20 June 2023

Statistical Bulletin 2022 published

The Bank has published the 2022 issue of its Statistical Bulletin: the som in circulation and reserve cover by month, the balance sheets of the licensed banks in summary and the turnover of the Clearing House.

The Bulletin is issued in print and may be consulted at the Library and Reading Room.
