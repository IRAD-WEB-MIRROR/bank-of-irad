27 June 2024

Annual Report 2023 published

The Bank of Irad has published its Annual Report for 2023. The report restates that only the United States dollar and the som are legal tender in the ISEZ, gives the reserve cover at the end of 2023 and reviews supervision and the payment system.

The report is issued in print. It is listed under Publications and statistics.
