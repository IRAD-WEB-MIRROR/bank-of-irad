3 September 2024

The payment system and the fixed rate are unaffected

In reply to questions from the press, the Bank of Irad states that the payment system is operating normally and that the fixed rate of 8.40 som to the dollar is unchanged. Salaries, pensions and payments between banks are settled as usual through the Clearing House.

The som remains fully covered by the Bank's reserves.

Press Office of the Bank of Irad
