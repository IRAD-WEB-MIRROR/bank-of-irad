Seminar for cashiers on the security features of the som

The Currency Issue and Cash Department held a two-day seminar for cashiers of the licensed banks and of Irad Pochtasy, which pays pensions at its post offices. Participants practised checking banknotes of the 2011 series and the handling of suspect notes.

A suspect banknote should not be passed on. Banks hand such notes to the Bank for examination.

Press Office of the Bank of Irad
