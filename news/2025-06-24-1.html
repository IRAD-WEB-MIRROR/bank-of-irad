24 June 2025

Statistical Bulletin 2024 published

The 2024 issue of the Statistical Bulletin has been published in print. It gives the som in circulation and reserve cover by month, a summary of the licensed banks' balance sheets and the turnover of the Clearing House.

Copies may be consulted at the Library and Reading Room.
