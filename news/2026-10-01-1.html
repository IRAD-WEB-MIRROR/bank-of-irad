1 October 2026

The Bank's pages move to bank.iradgov.org

The website of the Bank of Irad is now at bank.iradgov.org, on the government portal. It gives the official exchange rates, the currency board, the banknotes and coins, the notices of the Bank and the register of licensed institutions.

Pages of the Bank's former site in the id.gov zone are being transferred. Earlier press releases are collected on this page.
